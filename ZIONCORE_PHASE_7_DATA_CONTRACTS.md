## Data not persisted by these flows

- The inspected observation contracts store derived measurement metadata, not the microphone recording or imported audio bytes.
- Message encoding and browser playback can run locally; no persistent message archive was identified in the main Messages flow.
- Lab playback stores the encoded symbol list and playback metadata in a saved session, not a received response or audio waveform.
- Device/instrument registries describe adapters or imported measurements; they do not prove that physical sensors are connected.
- Owner dashboard summaries are computed from authorized business sources; no separate persistent dashboard snapshot was found.

## Ownership and access summary

1. **Customer research:** UID-scoped where cloud-backed; local browser data remains accessible to anyone with access to that browser profile. An account-scoped localStorage key is organization, not encryption or server authorization.
2. **Financial state:** Wallets, reservations, membership fulfillment, Stripe links, and billing review events are server-authoritative. Client SDK writes must not be used to adjust balances or entitlements.
3. **Owner privileges:** Must be verified by the server. No customer-controlled research record grants owner access.
4. **Production boundary:** The rules in both source refs are proposed source rules only. The deployed production rules, existing legacy collections, and all production data-retention settings remain uninspected.

## Gaps requiring resolution before broader cloud storage

- There is no account-wide export/deletion workflow covering observations, Signal Cards, research sessions, Stripe links, wallet/ledger history, provider-side records, and Firebase identity together.
- Research sessions containing prayers, hypotheses, and personal reflections are only browser-local in the task source, but the browser archive is unencrypted and may be cleared by device settings. Do not describe it as secure cloud storage.
- No retention schedule was found for observations, Signal Cards, wallet/credit records, Stripe references/events, or Firebase identity. Financial records must not be deleted by a generic customer-history cleanup.
- Firebase production paths, overlapping legacy rules, App Check exchange/enforcement, and provider data-retention terms are not verified. Do not migrate research sessions to Firestore or loosen client permissions based only on these checked-in rules.
- The main and lab observation contracts differ: the lab adds an optional interpretation field and more restricted App Check readiness. Any future merge must version and validate the contract without allowing interpretation updates to rewrite measured fields.

## Verification boundary

This report documents source contracts, not a compliance certification or live production audit. No customer data, provider payload, payment record, Firebase rule deployment, migration, or deletion was performed. Preserve the two source lines independently until their contracts and production rules are reconciled.
