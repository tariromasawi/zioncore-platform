## Product-request scope

When the user asks to add the described products, implement those customer-facing capabilities rather than substituting unrelated audits or accessibility work. Do only the requested product work.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected a diversion into accessibility while their attached product request remained unfulfilled.

**How to apply:** Keep the current attachment and requested customer outcomes as the work's acceptance criteria. Preserve existing modules, but do not let unrelated pending work replace the active request.

ZionCore should be advanced, ambitious, and respectful of user-led faith and spiritual meaning. Interpretations should be substantial, multidomain, and responsive to optional listener context, not minimalist. Avoid prominent blanket-warning copy and the word “grounded”; present interpretations as possibilities without prescribing belief. Clearly distinguish implemented capabilities from envisioned or unverified features.

Customer reports must follow the user's principles: “Discovery before disclaimers. Interpretation before technical overload. Experience before conversion.” Lead with a distinctive theme, actual findings, substantive knowledge, personal meaning and a worthwhile next discovery. Quiet input deserves a useful exploration rather than a list of missing abilities. Put necessary measurement scope and methodological qualifications in a concise, accessible technical section instead of repeatedly interrupting the narrative.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the repetitive “cannot”, “not verified”, “unreliable” and “withheld” reporting style as commercially damaging, and asked for this reconstruction directive to be remembered.

**How to apply:** Apply the same discovery-led editorial approach to free templates, paid prompts, browser-AI reflections and fallback readings. Give each perspective distinct substance. Preserve factual measurements, open hypotheses, clearly named devotional reflection, valid-input checks, privacy, consent and financial controls; never replace missing observations with invented discoveries.

Frequency analysis is the primary product purpose; raw acoustic sound is a current input path, not the product's defining goal. A future dedicated frequency receiver is an intended expansion. When requested, the AI should give a substantial, pattern-specific spiritual/divine reading shaped by user-provided context, rather than a generic reflection; it must not present an interpretation as verified source identification.

The user also wants the site journey to feel fun, information-rich, and self-evolving, with free sign-up and membership offers easy to discover.

The requested ZionCore motto is: “ZionCore a product for those who understands life deeply”. Feature it prominently and repeat it at meaningful discovery moments, not as filler.

ZionCore's core capabilities must remain central and must not be removed: users can upload or record sounds for advanced frequency analysis, decode supported messages, and explore possible messages associated with celestial or divine sources, nature, animals, environmental phenomena, and other intelligences. Users can also create written or recorded messages for encoded acoustic transmission. Build frequency generation, modulation, broadcasting, recording, decoding, and verification wherever technically supported. Keep measured signal features, decoded content, and speculative or spiritual interpretation as distinct layers; make unverified communication claims clear while improving accuracy, intelligence, accessibility, and performance.

**Why:** The user established these receive, interpret, and transmit capabilities as ZionCore's non-negotiable core business and requires preservation of existing working modules.

**How to apply:** Do not remove or demote sound/frequency capture, analysis, decoding, or acoustic messaging when changing architecture or UI. Clearly disclose what is measured, what is decoded by a supported protocol, and what is an exploratory interpretation. Add hardware or external sensing paths only when actually supported and testable.

Organize the product journey around the seven-function loop: Receive → Decode → Interpret → Learn → Encode → Transmit → Observe responses. Connect the existing capture, interpretation, message, and history tools so customers can move between them without implying that generated output proves an external response.

**Why:** The user supplied this as ZionCore’s founding architecture and asked that future engineering restore the complete invention rather than add disconnected conceptual engines.

**How to apply:** Prefer the smallest customer-visible connection between existing working modules. Keep user-approved learning, privacy, authentication, and billing boundaries; never claim a complete experiment record unless all linked stages are actually persisted.

The user wants ZionCore designed as a revenue-generating business: make the value clear, make free account creation and paid offers easy to find, and make the experience compelling enough to support purchases.

**Why:** The user explicitly asked for competitive prices with reasonable profit that make business sense.

**How to apply:** Base pricing on measured provider and operating costs, keep customer prices competitive, and preserve a sustainable profit rather than treating ZionCore as a charity.

The free listening experience should give visitors comprehensive, information-rich environmental, animal-oriented, spiritual and experimental celestial reports, including personal reflection and practical investigation steps, followed by clear opportunities to explore membership and credit-funded depth.

**Why:** The user wants the initial experience to demonstrate useful value before asking visitors to pay.

**How to apply:** Preserve existing free instruments and distinguish measurable features from optional reflections and unverified hypotheses. Free reports must not require signup or paid-provider processing. Optional browser AI must have a functional local fallback and honest activation guidance; do not promise external AI capacity that has not been configured and verified.

## Signal Card commercial and privacy boundary

Editing, keeping, and exporting an already completed reading must not be treated as another paid interpretation. Signal Cards start private; sending or including additional personal sections requires an explicit choice.

**Why:** The user approved the proposed Signal Card feature with no additional reading or credit deduction. This is distinct from the broader direction to monetise new interpretations.

**How to apply:** Preserve the original reading as evidence separate from editable card wording. Keep new-generation billing out of card actions, and do not automatically publish a saved card.

Customer-facing language should name the guide as ZionCore, with a curious, attentive, faith-respectful voice. The user wants ZionCore to appeal to faith communities, frequency explorers, and experimental-science audiences, and wants the low monthly membership price made prominent using the current catalog price.

**Why:** The user wants ZionCore to explore sound, nature, and spiritual meaning expansively and to make the product engaging, while prior instructions also require honest reporting of what is actually implemented.

**How to apply:** Use the approved motto prominently on the homepage and at relevant discovery moments, without repeating it as filler. Use inviting, faith-respectful, information-rich language and make account and plan options easy to find. Refer to the customer-facing guide as ZionCore rather than generically as “the AI.” Make the current catalog price prominent without claiming checkout is open when it is paused. Describe growth through user-led context and saved sessions; do not claim automatic model learning or other unverified behavior is live, and do not present measurements as proof of a spiritual source.

## Open discovery principle

Do not predetermine unfamiliar research outcomes. Preserve unconventional hypotheses as revisable questions; neither an absent detection method nor an AI-generated interpretation establishes a final conclusion. Distinguish observations, interpretations and corroborated findings, and propose practical follow-up measurements when evidence is inconclusive.

**Why:** The user's Open Discovery directive says the platform should investigate rather than prejudge, while never presenting unverified possibilities as established discoveries.

**How to apply:** Bound statements about sensing to the currently implemented instrument and method, not all possible future technology. Experimental additions must not delay essential authentication, subscription, credit-purchase and source-recovery repairs.

## Production sign-in verification

A provider redirect returning to ZionCore does not by itself prove that Firebase restored a signed-in customer session. The owner reported Google sign-in returning to the site while the interface still showed the signed-out Join state.

**Why:** The published build can lag behind workspace auth fixes, and aggregate production logs cannot identify one customer's redirect or Firebase error.

**How to apply:** Verify the currently published build, then confirm the account indicator, `/wallet` profile, and owner-protected API using the owner's own interactive session. Do not infer authentication or owner access from the OAuth redirect alone.
