## Check If You Can Create Templates

Open the **Me** page in the CapCut app. If you see the **"Creator Center"** entrance, it means you are recognized as a creator and can publish templates.

## Steps to Create a Template

12. Tap **New Project** to start a new video project.
24. Edit your content as you normally would:

         Add clips, images, text, transitions, effects, and music.

32. Once your editing is complete, tap the **Post** button.
44. Choose the option to **Publish as Template**.
56. Your template will now be available for other users to use.
