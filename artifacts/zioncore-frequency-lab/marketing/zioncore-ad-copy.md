## Campaign and claim guardrails

- Copy describes exploration and interpretation, not detection of threats, dark energy, divine messages, healing, protection, positive outcomes, or animal language.
- ZionCore analyzes supported microphone and local audio input. A microphone is not an RF or ultrasound receiver and does not exceed its hardware range.
- Saved observations support personal comparison; automatic model retraining is not enabled.
- Do not imply the measurement is a safety warning, a guaranteed all-clear, or a scientific diagnosis.
- The Premium catalog currently lists a low monthly price, but the offer is marked not purchasable and checkout is paused. **Do not run membership ad copy until checkout is enabled and the live offer is confirmed purchasable.** Any future price creative must use the current catalog value and state availability accurately.
- Keep ad landing-page claims aligned with current product behavior. These drafts are not published campaigns.
