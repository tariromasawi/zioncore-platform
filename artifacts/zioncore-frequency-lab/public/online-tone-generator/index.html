Generate a tone. Notice what your setup reveals.

ZionCore includes a simple online tone generator for short, user-directed listening and speaker comparison. Choose a frequency, play a controlled sine tone, then inspect the sound your microphone measures.

Open the tone controls Explore the analyzer

Choose familiar reference frequencies

The controls cover 100–8,000 Hz in one-hertz increments, with quick presets for 432 Hz, 440 Hz, and 528 Hz. You can choose a duration of one, three, or five seconds. Output gain is capped and the tone fades at the start and end.

Use a reference tone to test whether a speaker and microphone can reproduce a frequency, or to compare the pattern seen in the spectrum analyzer. The resulting measurement depends on the speaker, room, microphone, distance, and browser audio path.

Make listening comfortable

Start with your device volume low, play briefly, and increase only if comfortable. High tones can sound sharp or be inaudible depending on hearing and equipment. Stop playback if it feels uncomfortable.

This online tone generator is a reference instrument, not a calibrated laboratory signal source. Device volume, speaker response, room acoustics, and microphone processing all influence what reaches the analyzer.

Questions about ZionCore tone generation

Can I generate 432 Hz and 528 Hz?

Yes. Use the one-tap 432 Hz and 528 Hz presets or set the slider to any whole hertz value from 100 to 8,000 Hz.

Does a tone prove a healing or spiritual effect?

No frequency number guarantees a health or spiritual outcome. Listeners may bring personal meaning, prayer, or relaxation practices; ZionCore keeps those reflections distinct from measured sound.

Can I analyze the tone after playing it?

Yes. Play the reference through a speaker and start a microphone capture in the frequency lab to compare the measured spectrum. Room and device response will affect the result.
