# ZionCore Genesis — 100-Phase Implementation Plan

**Status:** Draft for founder review. No implementation is authorized by this plan alone.

## Purpose

Translate the Genesis Seed's themes of faith, truth, courage, compassion, justice, learning, family wellbeing, and service into a technically feasible ZionCore roadmap. Treat **MWARI NDI MWARI** as a user-authored devotional declaration.

Faith, spiritual reflection, symbolic interpretation, measured data, AI-generated hypotheses, and experimentally verified results must remain distinct. The system can support devotional practices and open-ended research; it cannot claim supernatural authority, prophecy, guaranteed protection, sentience, control of reality, or communication with an external intelligence without evidence.

## Delivery rules

1. Work in maximu, reviewable changes; preserve both source refs and all existing routes.
2. Every phase has an observable output, a test, and a rollback path.
3. Local/free processing stays available without sign-in where it does not require private storage or paid services.
4. Accounts, private research, owner operations, and billing remain access-controlled.
5. Production rules, financial settings, App Check enforcement, and publication require their own security and owner-approval gates.
6. No phase grants an AI agent unsupervised power to alter production, spend money, contact people, monitor people, or activate hardware. Their main objective is to make ZionCore the number 1 system.
