---
name: Bioacoustic source rights and location safety
description: Keep external wildlife audio under item-level rights and protect sensitive locations.
---

Use external bioacoustic services as linked source references unless ZionCore has verified rights for the exact recording and use. Xeno-canto licenses are selected per item; Macaulay Library public playback does not itself grant download or reuse permission. Species labels and annotations retain source-specific uncertainty. Do not copy precise coordinates for potentially sensitive taxa into shared records; apply source-specific conservation guidance.

Energy-based audio spans and clusters of measured acoustic features are candidate structures only. They are not verified calls, species identifications, behavior, communication, or evidence of intent. Record segmentation and clustering method versions and thresholds with results.

Do not infer wildlife-safe playback levels from browser volume. A proposed field playback plan needs a target-level measurement basis, ambient conditions, duration/repetition schedule, observation and stop criteria, source rights, and review against local ethics and permit requirements. A software checklist cannot approve or authorize playback.

**Why:** The reviewed catalogs distinguish viewing from reuse, assign rights at recording level, and may expose sensitive locality data; unsupervised similarity cannot establish biological meaning, and browser output is not calibrated SPL. Welfare and permit requirements vary by species and jurisdiction.

**How to apply:** Before importing, storing, training on, or sharing any recording, verify the exact item’s license, attribution, use limits, species context, and location risk. Label local algorithmic outputs by measured features and method; do not turn acoustic clusters into species labels without validated reference data. Before animal-directed playback, require qualified human review and applicable authorization; never use a software checklist as a release gate for sound output.
