## Source-only vision and unfinished scope

The following ambitions are not established as complete customer-facing services in either inspected source line: a global observation network, institutional multi-tenancy, an autonomous invention factory, a coordinated multi-agent council, validated cross-species translation, a production cosmic-data observatory, connected specialist hardware, and a general-purpose universal protocol foundry. Some related local registries, guides, experiments, or research utilities exist; they do not constitute those larger services.

Do not advertise these ambitions as completed capabilities. Preserve their source, label prototypes accurately, and integrate them only through reviewed, testable increments.

## Preservation baseline

1. Main-source candidate is the best-supported reference for the live main experience, but the exact deployed commit remains unverified.
2. The task-source line is a separate, preview-accessible research lab and must not replace the main experience.
3. The two refs differ substantially; no wholesale merge is safe.
4. For each row above, the named tests are preservation checks—not claims that the full live flow has passed. Production Firebase, Stripe, AI, and Storage operations remain separately gated.
5. No source code, live service, account, payment, or deployment was changed while creating this matrix.
