ZIONCORE PHASE 5 — OMNISENSE INFINITE SIGNAL PERCEPTION

Develop OmniSense into a comprehensive, modular sensing and signal acquisition platform.

Extend beyond ordinary recorded audio.

Implement support or extensible adapters for:

* Human and animal vocalisations.
* Natural environmental recordings.
* Hydrophone and underwater acoustic sources.
* Seismic and vibration measurements.
* Plant electrophysiology and biological sensor data.
* Electromagnetic and radio signal measurements.
* Astronomical public datasets and accessible observatory recordings.
* Multichannel, spatial, temporal, and high-resolution signals.
* Customer-supplied unconventional recordings.

Develop a shared computational signal format accommodating timestamps, spectral measurements, spatial context, calibration, units, uncertainty, provenance, and compatible metadata.

Implement adaptive noise reduction, event detection, time-frequency analysis, source separation where viable, anomaly detection, and recurring-pattern discovery.

Allow unfamiliar signals to remain unclassified while being thoroughly analysed.

Build real-time and asynchronous acquisition workflows.

Support expandable sensor plugins without granting unnecessary device permissions.

Develop rich visualisation tools: waveforms, spectrograms, frequency distributions, temporal maps, anomaly timelines, and cross-source comparisons.

Establish a future-ready sensor capability registry so unsupported hardware can be integrated when available.

Do not fabricate sensor data or invent measurements.

Make the customer’s first recording or upload immediately useful.

Preserve the existing audio pipeline and its functioning acoustic decoding technology.

Prioritise performance, privacy, accessible mobile interfaces, and efficient local signal processing.

The ambition is to make ZionCore a universal instrument capable of investigating measurable information across as many domains as technology allows.

EXPAND THE REACH OF PERCEPTION. BUILD WHAT DOES NOT YET EXIST.
