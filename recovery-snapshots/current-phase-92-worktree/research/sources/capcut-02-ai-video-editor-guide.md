## FAQ

### 1\. What is an AI video editor, and do I still need a timeline?

An AI video editor is a timeline-first tool that uses AI assists on existing footage—you still need a real timeline so you can correct pacing, captions, and packaging after the AI pass. CapCut keeps multi-track editing next to assists such as Auto Cut (where available) and Auto Captions, so the AI draft never has to be the final cut.

### 2\. What’s the best AI video editor for short-form edits in CapCut?

CapCut is a strong fit for short-form AI video editing when you already have footage for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, or similar feeds and want timeline control plus AI assists in one app. It is not a forever #1 claim for every editor or region—compare on timeline, Auto Captions, and platform export, not absolute free or unlimited marketing.

### 3\. Is there a free AI video editor that stays usable after export?

CapCut offers a free-to-start AI video editor path that stays usable for many first exports—import, timeline edit, and common AI assists when your account lists them. Free is not unlimited: after the first export, credits, Pro assets, or higher export settings may appear, and branding can depend on account and feature, so inspect the file. Soft “ai video editor free” means try the loop first, then check what your live account unlocks for client work.

### 4\. AI video editor vs AI video generator — which should I open first?

Open CapCut’s AI video editor first when you already have footage to cut, caption, and package. Open CapCut’s [AI video generator guide](https://www.capcut.com/resource/ai-video-generator-guide) first when the brief starts from a script or idea and you need scenes generated before polish. Many projects use both—generate a draft, then finish on the timeline—but the starting question is whether camera work already exists.

### 5\. Can beginners edit with AI in CapCut without a pro NLE?

Yes for beginners and small teams: CapCut’s AI video editing software path—Web, Desktop, or mobile—covers import, Auto Captions, and common polish without a traditional pro NLE. Start with a short clip set, one aspect ratio, and one assist (captions or Auto Cut on Mobile/Desktop), then learn manual trim on the same timeline. CapCut still expects a quick human review; AI assists draft, they do not replace taste.

### 6\. When should I lean on auto captions versus other AI assists?

Stay on the AI editor timeline when footage exists and you mainly need cuts, cleanup, and packaging. Lean harder into CapCut’s Auto Captions when speech clarity and on-screen text are the bottleneck—edit timing and wording after generate. Use Auto Cut only on Desktop or Mobile where listed; on Web, cut manually and keep captions plus other assists.
